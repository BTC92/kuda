1. Ko sam

2. Ukratko

Ja ne prikupljam vaše lične podatke. Nema registracije, naloga, reklama, analitike niti praćenja (tracking). Lokacija, omiljene stavke i pretrage ostaju na vašem telefonu. Jedini podaci koji napuštaju telefon su tehnički podaci potrebni za ažuriranja i prikaz mape (tačka 4).

3. Podaci koji ostaju samo na vašem uređaju

  • Lokacija dok koristite aplikaciju: samo ako dozvolite, da bi aplikacija prikazala najbliže stanice i isplanirala rutu.
  • Lokacija u pozadini („Kreni”): samo kada na određenoj vožnji dodirnete „Kreni” i dozvolite lokaciju „Uvek”. Telefon tada prati da li ste se približili stanici izlaska i šalje lokalno obaveštenje, i kada je aplikacija zatvorena. Praćenje se isključuje posle dolaska (najkasnije kada sledeći put otvorite aplikaciju).
  • Omiljene stanice/linije i nedavne pretrage: čuvaju se lokalno na telefonu.
  • Obaveštenja i podsetnici: lokalna obaveštenja koja zakazuje sam telefon; aplikacija ne koristi push servere.

Lokacija se obrađuje isključivo na telefonu i ne šalje se meni niti trećim licima. Ove podatke možete obrisati u aplikaciji ili brisanjem aplikacije. Dozvole za lokaciju i obaveštenja možete u svakom trenutku povući u Podešavanjima iOS-a.

4. Internet veze

Radi provere ažuriranja, aplikacija šalje servisu Expo (EAS Update, 650 Industries, Inc., SAD) tehničke podatke: platformu, verziju aplikacije, kanal ažuriranja, ID trenutnog ažuriranja, nasumični identifikator instalacije koji nije povezan sa vama i, posle pada aplikacije, kratku poruku o grešci. Vaša IP adresa je, kao kod svakog internet zahteva, vidljiva serveru. Lokacija, omiljene stavke i pretrage se ne šalju. Više: Expo Privacy Policy.

Ažuriranje reda vožnje (GitHub)

Aplikacija može povremeno da preuzme potpisanu datoteku sa redom vožnje sa servisa GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., SAD). Zahtev ne sadrži vašu lokaciju, omiljene stavke, pretrage niti bilo kakav identifikator. Kao i svaki veb-server, GitHub pri tome vidi vašu IP adresu i, prema sopstvenim pravilima, beleži je radi bezbednosti. Ja nemam pristup tim zapisima. Više: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Mape (Apple)

Mapa u aplikaciji koristi Apple Maps. Apple obrađuje zahteve za prikaz mape prema politici privatnosti kompanije Apple (apple.com/legal/privacy). Ja ne dobijam te podatke.

Privacy Policy – Kuda (English)

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

1. Who I am

Kuda is developed and published by a private individual, Stefan Radojević, Kragujevac, Serbia ("I"). Contact: stefanradojevictm@gmail.com. Kuda is a non-commercial project and is not an official app of any transport operator or public authority.

2. In short

I do not collect your personal data. There is no sign-up, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location, favourites and searches stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is technical data needed for updates and to show the map (section 4).

3. Data that stays on your device only

  • Location while using the app: only if you allow it, so the app can show the nearest stops and plan a route.
  • Background location ("Go"): only when you tap "Go" on a specific trip and allow location "Always". Your phone then checks whether you are approaching your stop and shows a local notification, even when the app is closed. Monitoring stops after you arrive (at the latest the next time you open the app).
  • Favourite stops/lines and recent searches: stored locally on your phone.
  • Notifications and reminders: local notifications scheduled by your phone; the app uses no push server.

Location is processed on your phone only and is never sent to me or to third parties. You can delete this data in the app or by deleting the app, and withdraw location and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Internet connections

App updates (Expo). To check for app updates, the app sends Expo (EAS Update, 650 Industries, Inc., USA) technical data: platform, app/runtime version, update channel, current update IDs, a random installation identifier not linked to you, and, after a crash, a short error message. Your IP address is visible to the server as with any internet request. No location, favourites or searches are sent. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

Timetable updates (GitHub). The app may periodically download a signed timetable file from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). The request contains no location, favourites, searches or identifiers. Like any web server, GitHub sees your IP address and, under its own policy, logs it for security purposes. I have no access to these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Maps (Apple). The map uses Apple Maps. Apple processes map requests under Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). I do not receive that data.

5. When you email me

If you email me, I process your email address and message only to reply (legal basis: legitimate interest – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 12(1)(6) of the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, ZZPL). I delete messages no later than 12 months after the conversation ends, unless the law requires otherwise.

6. No tracking, no ads

There are no ads, analytics, profiling or automated decision-making. Data is not sold or shared for advertising. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the new app version is released.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of data I hold about you (in practice only emails). Write to stefanradojevictm@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Serbia the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (www.poverenik.rs); in Sweden/EU Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY, www.imy.se), or the authority in the country where you live.

8. Children

The app is not directed specifically at children and collects no data about children.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
